Refund Policy

StegMS products are digital and are delivered immediately, so purchases are not refunded simply for a change of mind. We do refund when we fall short: when something you paid for does not work as described, when the service is unavailable for a significant time, when you are charged in error, or when our security fails you.

How to ask

Email support@stegms.com with your receipt or order reference (and your username, for a Hosted Account) and what went wrong. Purchases are sold through Link (Stripe), which provides the receipt and order history; you can also contact Link support about a purchase. We aim to answer within 2 business days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method.

When we refund on request

  • StegMS Pro for desktop ($14.99 one-time). A full refund if a Pro feature described for your platform does not work on a supported device, and we cannot fix it or give you a working alternative within 14 days of your report.
  • Hosted Account ($7.99 per month). A refund of the current month if the service was unavailable or materially not working for more than 2 consecutive days, or more than 5 days in total in that month.
  • Billing errors. A duplicate charge, a charge after you cancelled, or a charge for something you did not order is refunded in full.

When we refund without being asked

  • When we find a billing error ourselves.
  • When a security incident on our side materially exposes your Hosted Account data: we refund the month in which it happened and the following month and tell you what happened.
  • If we stop offering the Hosted Account service: we refund the unused part of your current paid month.

What is not refunded

A month of a Hosted Account that was used normally, and a cancellation part-way through a month: when you cancel, access continues to the end of the month you paid for and you are not charged again. Pro is not refunded for a change of mind once delivered.

What a refund changes

A refunded Hosted Account month ends access for that month. A refunded Pro purchase ends the Pro license; because a signed license works offline, an already activated installation cannot always be disabled remotely, but that does not preserve a right to use Pro after a refund.

Android

Purchases made through Google Play are refunded through Google Play’s processes.

Your legal rights

This policy does not affect your statutory rights. Where the law gives you a right to cancel or withdraw from a purchase, or to a remedy for faulty digital content or services, that right applies in addition to this policy.
