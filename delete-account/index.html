Delete your account

You can delete your StegMS Hosted Account here, without the app. In the app, the same option is in Settings > Danger Zone > Delete My Account Permanently.

Can't sign in?

If you have forgotten your password or cannot use the form, email support@stegms.com with the subject “Account deletion request” and your username. We will confirm the account is yours before deleting it, and reply when it is done. Never send us your password.

What is deleted

  • Your account, contacts, reactions and settings.
  • Your encryption keys, profile picture, and the attachments you sent.
  • Your sign-in sessions, trusted devices and push notification registrations.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Messages you sent stay in the other members' conversations, shown as from “Deleted User”, so they keep their history.
  • Billing records are kept for 90 days after deletion. Stripe keeps its own payment records under its policy.
  • Backups are kept for 7 days, so deleted data is gone from them within 7 days. Deleted attachments are removed from storage within a few days.
  • A valid legal hold can require specific records to be kept longer.

Local Cwtch and guest use

Local Cwtch profiles and guest sessions are not StegMS accounts: their data is kept only on your device, not on our servers. To delete it, delete the profile in the app, or clear the app's data or uninstall it.

StegMS Pro on Android

StegMS Pro bought on Google Play belongs to your Google account, not to a StegMS account; Google keeps the purchase record. See the Privacy Policy for details.
