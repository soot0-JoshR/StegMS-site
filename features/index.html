Who sees what: Cwtch and Hosted chats

Both keep message contents end-to-end encrypted. The difference is what passes through StegMS. Each chat is one or the other, and you can use both in the same app.

Limits to know about

  • Image and WAV modes conceal encrypted data in lossless pixel or sample values. They hide content from casual inspection; they are not a guarantee of undetectability against forensic analysis. Resizing, recompression, transcoding, normalization, trimming, or sanitization may destroy hidden data.
  • Higher bit depths hold more data but change the cover file more.
  • Video mode appends an encrypted StegMS payload to the file. It protects the contents but is not covert against format-aware inspection.
  • Hidden text characters do not display, but they can be found by inspecting the text.
  • Burning relies on the apps in the chat. It cannot erase screenshots, exports or copies held by a modified app.
  • Delayed reveal is enforced by the app, so a modified app could get around it.
  • A hidden carrier is limited to 16 MiB, on Free and Pro.
