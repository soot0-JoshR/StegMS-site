Privacy Policy

StegMS provides distinct Local Cwtch and optional Hosted Account modes. StegMS does not operate advertising or behavioral analytics.

Account and privacy choices

Local Cwtch

Local Cwtch accounts minimize centralized metadata and third-party exposure. Identity, contacts, messaging, and media transfer use the local Cwtch runtime and Tor without a StegMS hosted messaging account.

Hosted Account

Hosted Accounts trade some metadata privacy and add subpoena or other lawful-request exposure in exchange for managed availability, synchronization across your devices, and encrypted attachment storage.

Attachment contents remain end-to-end encrypted under both models. For Hosted Accounts, the client encrypts an attachment before upload and the storage provider holds encrypted data. Storage and network providers may still observe operational metadata such as encrypted object size, timing, IP address, and access activity.

Data on your device

Cwtch profiles, onion identities, contacts, messages, media, preferences, cryptographic material, and StegMS vaults may be stored locally. Persistent data remains until you delete it, remove a profile, clear app data, or uninstall; ephemeral profiles are intended to be destroyed when their session ends. Backups or operating-system behavior may affect deletion.

On installed apps, the App Lock PIN is checked on the device and its verifier is kept in the operating system's protected storage. A trusted-device credential that lets a device skip repeated sign-in checks is also kept in that protected storage.

Local Cwtch and Tor

Local Cwtch messaging connects through Cwtch and Tor. Network peers and infrastructure necessarily process ordinary network traffic; StegMS does not receive a hosted copy of Local Cwtch messages.

Hosted Account data

The Hosted Account service stores the registered account (username and a salted password hash), entitlement and subscription state, contacts and contact requests, conversations and their membership, and the messages you send. The visible text of a hosted message is stored as sent and is readable by the service; it is not end-to-end encrypted. Hidden (steganographic) content in hosted messages and attachments are encrypted on your device before upload, and the service does not receive the keys to decrypt them. The service also stores delivery, read, reaction and deletion state, your public encryption keys, and, if you use a browser App Lock with a Hosted Account, a hash of that PIN. Payment and infrastructure providers can associate their own transaction, account, request, and network records with StegMS operations.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the service stores your device's push subscription (the push service address and keys, the platform and the browser or device description) and sends notifications through that push service: Google Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android, and your browser vendor's push service on the web. Notifications contain only generic text such as “New private message” and a conversation reference, never message content; the push service still sees that a notification was sent to your device and when.

Service logs

The service keeps operational logs for reliability and security. They are designed not to record message content or who messages whom, but hosting providers process IP addresses and request metadata for every connection. Service logs are kept by our hosting provider for about 7 days, then deleted. We do not copy them elsewhere.

Legal requests and deletion

StegMS and its service providers may be required to preserve or disclose records in response to valid legal process. End-to-end encryption limits the usefulness of stored encrypted content, but it does not conceal visible message text, account, payment, traffic, timing, object-size, or relationship metadata. Deleting a Hosted Account also cancels its subscription. What deletion removes, and how long anything else is kept, is set out below. A valid legal hold can require specific records to be kept longer, and copies other people made (such as screenshots or exports) are outside our control.

How long we keep data

  • Account, contacts and settings: until you delete your account.
  • Messages: until they are deleted for everyone or burned. A conversation is removed from the server, with its messages and attachments, once every member has deleted it or left it.
  • When you delete your account: your account, contacts, reactions and settings are deleted, and your attachments and profile picture are removed. Messages you sent stay in the other members' conversations, shown as from “Deleted User”, so they keep their history.
  • Sign-in sessions: the lifetime you choose (1 to 90 days), or until you sign out.
  • Billing records: while your account exists, and for 90 days after you delete it. Stripe keeps its own payment records under its policy.
  • Deleted attachments: removed from storage within a few days.
  • Service logs: about 7 days.
  • Backups: taken daily and kept for 7 days, so deleted data is gone from backups within 7 days.

Children

StegMS is not directed at children under 13, and Hosted Accounts are for adults aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has created a Hosted Account, contact us and we will delete it.

Device permissions

Camera access is used for QR scanning when you request it. File access is used to open, encode, decode, save, or share files you select. Notifications are shown according to your settings.

Purchases

Google processes Android purchases under Google Play. Stripe processes Hosted Account and Electron purchases, receipts, taxes, refunds, and related transaction records under its own policies. Purchases are sold through Link (Stripe). If you ask Stripe to delete your purchase data, Stripe also cancels your Hosted Account subscription.

Website

GitHub Pages processes ordinary request data such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, and time when serving this static site.
