Privacy Policy

StegMS has no hosted messaging account and does not operate telemetry, advertising, analytics, a mailing list, or customer profiles.

Data on your device

Cwtch profiles, onion identities, contacts, messages, media, preferences, cryptographic material, and StegMS vaults are stored locally. Persistent data remains until you delete it, remove a profile, clear app data, or uninstall; ephemeral profiles are intended to be destroyed when their session ends. Backups or operating-system behavior may affect deletion.

Cwtch and Tor

Native messaging connects through Cwtch and Tor. Network peers and infrastructure necessarily process ordinary network traffic; StegMS does not receive a hosted copy of your Cwtch messages.

Device permissions

Camera access is used for QR scanning when you request it. File access is used to open, encode, decode, save, or share files you select. Local notifications may display events on your device according to your settings.

Purchases

Google processes Android Pro purchases under Google Play. Lemon Squeezy processes Electron purchases, receipts, taxes, refunds, and its generated license keys under its own policies.

Website and activation

GitHub Pages processes ordinary request data such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, and time when serving this static site. Cloudflare processes ordinary network metadata when protecting the stateless activation endpoint. Activation transmits the Lemon license key to Cloudflare and Lemon Squeezy for validation. It does not transmit your Cwtch identity, messages, contacts, cryptographic keys, vault, files, or profile nickname. The service returns a signed license containing only a pseudonymous license identifier, issuance time, product, schema, and signing-key ID; it has no customer database.
