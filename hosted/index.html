Hosted Account

A Hosted Account adds managed availability: your messages, contacts and encrypted attachments are kept by the StegMS service and reach your devices when you sign in. It trades some privacy for that convenience; Local Cwtch remains free and does not need an account.

Sign up

What you get

  • Sign in across your devices, including in the browser at app.stegms.com
  • Delivery when the other person is offline
  • Messages and attachments kept for you, end-to-end encrypted
  • Groups of up to 50 people
  • Push notifications on Android and the web (not on desktop)

A Hosted Account does not include StegMS Pro.

Billing

$7.99 USD per month, renewing automatically each month until cancelled. Cancelling stops the next renewal; access continues to the end of the paid period. If a renewal payment fails, access continues for a 7-day grace period. See the Terms and Refund Policy.

What the Hosted service stores

For Hosted chats only. Cwtch chats do not go through the Hosted service, even if you also have a Hosted Account.

  • Your messages and attachments, encrypted on your device. The service does not receive the keys to read them.
  • Your account, contacts, conversations and their membership.
  • Information the service can read: usernames, profile pictures, group names and reaction emoji, plus who messaged whom, when, message and file sizes, file types, and that a message has a burn policy.

This encryption has no forward secrecy, and the service distributes the public keys it relies on, so it is not a guarantee against a malicious or compromised service.

See the Privacy Policy for detail.

Prefer not to have an account? Local Cwtch messaging runs over Tor directly between devices, without a StegMS account, and StegMS never receives those messages or who you talk to. Compare Cwtch and Hosted, or see Downloads.
